# Blueprint claims intake — healthcare demo

## What this demo is for
A short executive-facing concept demo showing how quickly Blueprint can produce
a working claims intake process. Audience is C-suite or senior business leads in
healthcare or adjacent regulated industries. Duration target is 5 minutes — keep it tight.

## How this was generated
This demo was generated via the Blueprint MCP, not hand-coded. The source Blueprint
app ID is recorded in demo.yaml. To regenerate:

1. Open Claude with the Blueprint MCP connected
2. Pull the source app using the Blueprint MCP
3. Prompt: "Using this Blueprint app output, generate a self-contained HTML demo
   that shows the claims intake flow for an executive audience. 5 minutes guided.
   No technical detail — show outcomes and speed of design."

## The scenario
A healthcare payer needs to modernise their claims intake process. Blueprint was used
to design the new process in a single session. The demo shows:
1. The intake form — clean, AI-assisted field mapping
2. The process map Blueprint produced — visual, not config
3. The outcome: "from blank page to deployable process design in under 2 hours"

## Tone and framing
- Speak to speed and confidence, not technology
- The audience does not need to understand what Blueprint is — show the result first,
  name the product second
- Avoid jargon: "case type", "data model", "ruleset" — use "process", "workflow", "design"

## Design decisions
- Clean, light, clinical aesthetic — white with teal accents
- Large typography, minimal screens — this is a leave-behind concept, not a click-through
- The Blueprint logo/name appears only on the final slide
